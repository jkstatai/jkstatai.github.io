In 2025, I was honored to receive the 2024–25 Master’s Teaching Assistant of the Year Award from Duke Statistical Science. This award was presented in recognition of excellence in student teaching.

Throughout my time at Duke, I was grateful for the opportunity to support students through teaching, office hours, and one-on-one guidance. Being recognized in this way is especially meaningful to me, and I deeply appreciate the faculty, students, and department for their support.
