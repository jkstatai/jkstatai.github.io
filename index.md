Hi y’all, welcome to my page! I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at the <a href="https://statistics.rice.edu/" target="_blank">Department of Statistics, George R. Brown School of Engineering and Computing, Rice University</a>. My research lies at the intersection of foundation models (tabular), pre-trained models, neural network architectures, machine learning, Bayesian methods, MCMC, and Gaussian processes (hierarchical/empirical Bayes), with additional interests in predictive inference and differential privacy. I am broadly interested in applications to engineering problems, climate and environmental research (methodological development and analysis), quantitative finance, and patient cost prediction (in collaboration with Baylor College of Medicine).

Currently, I work with <a href="https://meng.rice.edu/" target="_blank">Dr. Meng Li</a> at Rice University on _Tabular Foundation Model_ and _Hierarchical Bayes Optimal Posterior Contraction for Gaussian Process Derivative Functionals_.

Previously, I worked with <a href="https://www2.stat.duke.edu/~jerry/" target="_blank">Dr. Jerry Reiter</a> at Duke University on _Bayesian and frequentist intervals under differential privacy for binomial proportions_, published in <a href="https://jkstatai.github.io/publications/s42519-026-00617-9.pdf" target="_blank">JSTP</a>.

In addition to my academic research, I also enjoy collaborating on computational and methodological projects. Recently, I worked with my Duke colleagues <a href="https://hollyyfc.github.io/" target="_blank">Holly</a> and Alex on a side project involving statistical computing and applied modeling. More details will be announced soon.
